About this work

"The famous haunted tower is up for sale" — Kuroiwa Ruikō's central adaptation begins from that line. Based on Mrs Williamson's A Woman in Grey, it kept the English setting and merely gave the characters Japanese names, and ran in the Yorozu Chōhō. Treasure said to lie beneath the clock tower, a murder committed there in the past, the enigmatic beauty Hideko, an insectary where numberless spiders are kept. The speed with which one device follows another is astonishing, and Japanese readers loved it so well that Edogawa Ranpo later rewrote it himself. Countless writers first shivered over it as boys.

Summary

My uncle Marube Asao, who has retired from the office of procurator general and lives at leisure, announces that he will buy the "Ghost Tower" that has come up for sale. The Ghost Tower was once the mansion of a family of the same name as my uncle's, and had passed into other hands with their decline. "I" — his nephew Marube Michikurō — am entrusted with the whole business of the purchase, and setting foot in the ruined house I meet there a beautiful and mysterious woman, Hideko. She seems to know the secret of the tower, and seems also to have given a ruffian named Gonda the right of command over her person; and their relation is one I cannot in any way make out. ... There is a legend about the house that an immense treasure is hidden at the foot of the clock tower, and in the past a murder was committed over that treasure. A cipher showing where the treasure lies, the "secret charge" Hideko carries, the pursuit of the detective Mori Mondo, and Madame Torai, who keeps an uncanny insect garden in which she breeds countless spiders. Swaying between his love for Hideko and his doubt of her, I pass through one trap and one crisis after another. At the end Madame Torai vanishes, leaving her lemur behind, and it is learned that she has fled to Australia with her brother Anagawa Jinzō and a bachelor of medicine. The insect garden is left empty, and hundreds and thousands of spiders spin their webs there. And as for the treasure at the foot of the tower — by right of blood it was neither mine nor my uncle's, but wholly Haruko's.

Characters

  • I (Marube Michikurō)　The nephew entrusted with the purchase of the Ghost Tower
  • Marube Asao　The uncle, a former procurator general, who buys the Ghost Tower
  • Hideko (Haruko)　The mysterious beauty who knows the secret of the tower
  • Madame Torai　The mistress of the insect garden, who breeds countless spiders
  • Mori Mondo　The detective who follows the case in disguise

The opening (original text)

Part one: A tremendous treasure "The famous Ghost Tower is up for sale; it is in the newspaper advertisements." Before many people were yet talking of it, the one who at once conceived the idea of buying it was my uncle Marube Asao, who has retired from the office of procurator general and lives at leisure.
