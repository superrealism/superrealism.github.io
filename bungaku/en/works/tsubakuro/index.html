About this work

"I hesitated a little over whether to tell you" — Kihira Takao cannot make the unexpected news brought by his friend feel real. Attacked at the old archery ground on a night of clear moonlight, he realizes that he knew nothing at all about Oichi, the wife of four years at his side. The ending, in which he says to his child that when it grows warm the swallows will come back too, quietly discloses what the title means.

Summary

Because Kira's news was so sudden and so far from anything he had expected, Kihira Takao could not at once take it in as real. "I wondered a little whether to tell you, but it is not like other things," says Kira Setsutarō, keeping his tone deliberately light — and the story begins from that one scene. ... On a night of a clear moon Takao was walking the road past the old archery ground. It is some twenty thousand tsubo in extent and used now for storing stone and timber, ringed about like a fence with old chinquapins and oaks. Stopping to put out his lantern because the moon was so bright, he felt behind him the presence of something not ordinary; and turning by reflex, the body of a masked man and the flash of a sword coming down at his head filled his eyes. He threw the lantern and sprang aside. ... The meaning of the attack, and the meaning of what he has been told, Takao must make out with his own hands. And in the course of it what he is brought to know is his own ignorance of his wife. This hand held O-Ichi, this skin touched her skin — but did he truly hold her, truly touch her? It was not so; as the wind passing through a wood only grazes the surface of the trees, he had only grazed her surface. "Yes, I do not know the person called O-Ichi; four years married, and I do not know her in the least." At the close, feeling something like hot water welling in his breast, Takao whispers to his child almost in tears: "When it grows warm, the swallows will come back, and Dai-san's mother will come back too.... Only a little more patience; when the winter is over and spring comes."

Characters

  • Kihira Takao　A man brought to know that he never knew his wife
  • O-Ichi　Takao's wife of four years
  • Kira Setsutarō　The friend who brings the unexpected news
  • Daisuke　The child to whom Takao speaks
