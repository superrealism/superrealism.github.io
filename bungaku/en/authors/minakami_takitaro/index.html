Biography

Born in Azabu, Tokyo, in 1887, the fourth son of Abe Taizō, founder of the Meiji Life Insurance Company; his real name was Abe Shōzō. He won recognition while a student at Keio University with "Yamanote no Ko" ("A Child of the Yamanote"), and became a disciple of Nagai Kafū. After studying in Europe and America he stood as a businessman, serving as managing director of Meiji Life, while acting as the spiritual pillar of "Mita Bungaku" and fostering younger writers. He left works shot through with a strong moral sense and a cool criticism of civilization, among them "Ōsaka no Yado" ("An Inn at Osaka") and "Kaigara Tsuihō" ("Banishment of the Shell"). He died in 1940 at the age of 52.
