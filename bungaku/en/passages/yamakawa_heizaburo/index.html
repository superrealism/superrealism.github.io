The Divine Comedy

Before me there were no created things, Only eterne, and I eternal last. All hope abandon, ye who enter in!

The close of the inscription carved over the gate of Hell in Canto III of the Inferno. The line “All hope abandon” is quoted the world over as the words that mark the entrance to despair; the inscription is also quoted in Sōseki's “The Tower of London.”
