About this work

A long science-fiction novel Unno Jūza serialized in Radio Kagaku in 1936, a scientific adventure story the pioneer of Japanese science fiction wrote with young readers in mind. From the uncanny appearance of a giant tortoise-like beetle at the village of Yaoi, through the secret of the monsters the mad Dr Tsujikawa has built, to a great conspiracy that shakes the world — the boy Takeo, Omiyo, Bachelor Ōsumi and the reporter Sasa face a wildly inventive affair. A rousing tale of imaginary science.

Summary

The boy Kawamura Takeo and Omiyo, climbing a honey locust tree to hunt for beetles, come upon a monstrous beetle as huge as a turtle — twice the size of a lunchbox. It is a bizarre creature, not seeming to belong to this world at all, calling to mind the Loch Ness Monster. In time, the mystery of this giant creature turns out to connect to a series of huge, ogre-like beings appearing one after another in Yaoi village. Behind it all is the mad scientist Dr. Tsujikawa, living in a house so bizarre that its like cannot be found anywhere else in Japan. In an iron cage in the doctor's garden, nothing was gathered but stark-naked giant humans as large as pigs, and one monstrous giant creature after another. Ōsumi, a scholar, and Sasa, a newspaper reporter who pilots the airship Sky Louse, team up and sneak into Dr. Tsujikawa's mansion to expose his terrible secret. Will they manage to rescue Takeo, who has been captured and transformed almost beyond recognition? Bachelor Ōsumi and the others boldly stand up against a great conspiracy that shakes the world — a scientific adventure tale of Unno Jūza, the pioneer of Japanese science fiction, full of wildly inventive devices and rousing adventure. The story closes with a voice murmuring, looking up at the moon, "Everyone's happy now, aren't they."

Characters

  • Kawamura Takeo　A boy who encounters the bizarre giant beetle. Later captured and transformed by Dr. Tsujikawa
  • Omiyo　A girl close to Takeo
  • Bachelor Ōsumi　A scholar investigating the strange affair. Teams up with reporter Sasa to sneak into Dr. Tsujikawa's mansion
  • Reporter Sasa　A newspaper reporter who pilots the airship Sky Louse
  • Dr. Tsujikawa　A mad scientist who has created monstrous giant creatures
