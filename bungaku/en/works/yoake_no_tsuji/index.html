About this work

More than forty people — senior officials, roughs and women alike — gather for a lecture at the residence of the chief retainer. Argument over the doctrines of Yamagata Daini turns in time into a political whirlpool that shakes the domain. It is only after the event that the young Michinoshin understands what Kunugi Ihei's words meant. A long novel closing on a dawn scene in which the birds in the pine wood begin to raise a note of blessing.

Summary

When Kunugi Ihei went in, the lecture had already begun. In a room made by taking down the sliding doors between a twenty-mat and a ten-mat room, some forty listeners were crowded in. There were the elders, beginning with the domain's chief minister Tsuda Tanomo; old men one would not have expected; rough young men with nothing to do with learning; and in a corner of the lower seats four or five women listening earnestly — the story begins from this strange sight. What is being expounded is the doctrine of Yamagata Daini. ... That night Ihei abused Daini's doctrine and said, "Yamagata will be killed before long." To the young Michinoshin the words were a riddle; but the day came when he learned their meaning. Within the domain a secret order had gone out to "deal with them under the name of desertion and private quarrel" — seven men who had come to that place without giving notice. "That is... a heavy charge," someone answers. Learning and politics, loyalty and duty, and what it means for a man to cut down a man, are set before the young men. At dawn, when it is all over, Michinoshin faces Sawa. "I shall come back" — those were his last words. He began to walk with his eyes fixed on hers, and presently quickened his pace; but each time he looked back their eyes were joined hotly. The road turns downhill and his figure is hidden. Straight along the road Ihei had taken, he too went away eastward, eastward. The sun was higher still; in the pine wood the birds had begun to raise their voices of blessing; and the mountains with snow on their peaks were already telling faintly, under a pale green sky, of the budding of spring.

Characters

  • Kunugi Ihei　The mysterious man who abused Daini's doctrine
  • Michinoshin　The young man who later learns what Ihei's words meant
  • Sawa　The woman who sees Michinoshin off
  • Tsuda Tanomo　The domain's chief minister, who arranged the lecture
