Famous passages by Lafcadio Hearn

Well-known lines from the works of Lafcadio Hearn.

3 passages

The Story of Mimi-nashi-Hōichi

Here is the biwa; but of the biwa-player I see — only two ears!

Hōichi, his whole body covered with sutras, is invisible to the ghost; only his ears, which were forgotten, float in the darkness. These words, spoken by the ghostly Heike warrior who has come for him, form the climax of the tale.

Yuki-Onna

But, if you ever tell anybody — even your own mother — about what you have seen this night, I shall know it; and then I will kill you.

The words the snow woman leaves behind after sparing only Minokichi's life in the blizzard-bound hut. The story holds its quiet tension until the day this promise is broken.

Mujina

“Hé! Was it anything like THIS that she showed you?” cried the soba-man, stroking his own face — which therewith became like unto an Egg.... And, simultaneously, the light went out.

The close of the story: a double twist in which the merchant who has fled from a faceless woman is shown the same face by the soba seller he turns to for help. The brief ghost story ends in a sudden blackout.
