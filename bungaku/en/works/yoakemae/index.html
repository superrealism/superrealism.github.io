About this work

"The Kiso road runs all through the mountains" — Shimazaki Tōson's last long novel, and one of the central historical novels of modern Japan. Modelled on his father, who kept the official inn and headmanship at Magome on the Kiso road, it covers the thirty years from the end of the shogunate into the Meiji era through the life of Aoyama Hanzō, a man who lived by the Hirata school of national learning. It is the story of a scholar who dreamed of the Restoration as a daybreak, and was betrayed by the age that came. Carried in four volumes.

Characters

  • Aoyama Hanzō　Heir to the official inn, the headmanship, and the forwarding agency of Magome in Kiso, and a follower of the national learning of the Hirata school
  • Aoyama Kichizaemon　Hanzō's father, the head of the house in the previous generation
  • O-Tami　Hanzō's wife
  • O-Man　Hanzō's stepmother
  • Fushimiya Kinbei　An elder of Magome
  • Miyagawa Kansai　Hanzō's teacher in learning
  • Katsushige　Hanzō's pupil
