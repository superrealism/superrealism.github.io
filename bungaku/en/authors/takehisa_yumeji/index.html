Biography

Born in 1884 into a sake-brewing family in Oku District, Okayama Prefecture; his real name was Shigejirō. While studying at Waseda Jitsugyō School he taught himself to draw and won recognition for his magazine vignette illustrations. His melancholy "Yumeji-style beauties" caused a sensation, and, working across postcards, chiyogami paper, sheet-music covers, and book design, he became emblematic of the Taishō Romantic era. As a poet, too, he left many poems and children's songs, beginning with the lyrics for "Yoimachigusa" ("The Evening Primrose"), and published illustrated poetry books such as "Sakura Saku Shima" ("The Island Where Cherries Bloom") and "Dontaku." He lived a life colored by love and wandering, and died in 1934 at the Fujimi Kōgen sanatorium in Shinshū, at the age of 49.
