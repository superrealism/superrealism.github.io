What is Aozora (coterie magazine)?

Category
Society / Group

A coterie magazine founded in January 1925, centered on Tokyo Imperial University students including Kajii Motojirō, Nakatani Takao, and Tomura Shigeru. The title is said to derive from a clear blue sky the founders had seen and from a line in a poem by Mushanokōji Saneatsu: "Let those who would clamor, clamor — I have the blue sky." Kajii's "Lemon," carried in the inaugural issue, along with signature works such as "In a Town with a Castle" in the second issue and "K's Ascension" and "A Winter Day," were all published here, and Yodono Ryūzō, Miyoshi Tatsuji, Kitagawa Fuyuhiko, and Abe Tomoji later joined as well. Kajii and Nakatani had been close friends since their days at the Third Higher School, and the founding group numbered only six. All were unknown young men, and the magazine itself folded in June 1927 after 28 issues in all; it was only after Kajii's death that his name became widely known. In embodying, in the last years of the Taishō era, the tradition by which unknown writers outside the literary establishment sharpened their skills through a small coterie magazine and from there emerged as writers, this one publication stands out. Kajii's characteristic style — a keen sensibility fixed unwaveringly on the things around him — was nurtured on the pages of this small magazine.
