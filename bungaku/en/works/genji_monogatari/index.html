About this work

Written by Murasaki Shikibu in the mid-Heian period, in fifty-four chapters, and among the oldest long novels in the world. It follows the loves and the splendour of Hikaru Genji — an emperor's son reduced to commoner rank — and then the fortunes of his descendants, across the reigns of four emperors and more than seventy years. Its court ceremonies and its fineness about men and women, carried by a prose famous for the quality called mono no aware, have shaped Japanese writing ever since. This app carries the modern Japanese version by the poet Yosano Akiko, in fifty-six volumes.
