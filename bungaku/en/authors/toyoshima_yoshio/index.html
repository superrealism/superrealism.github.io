Biography

Born in 1890 in Fukuda Village, Shimotsuza District, Fukuoka Prefecture (now Asakura City). He studied French literature at Tokyo Imperial University, founded the third series of "Shinshichō" with Akutagawa Ryūnosuke and Kikuchi Kan, and entered the literary world with "Kosui to Karera" ("The Lake and They"). He is known for his weighty translations of Hugo's "Les Misérables" and Romain Rolland's "Jean-Christophe," and contributed greatly to the introduction of French literature in Japan. While teaching as a professor at Hōsei University, he also left fine limpid children's stories such as "Tenka Ichi no Uma" ("The Finest Horse Under Heaven"). He died in 1955 at the age of 64.
