Biography

Born in Akasaka, Tokyo, in 1913. In 1932, while a student at Waseda University, he competed as a rower at the Los Angeles Olympics. He is known for "Orinposu no Kajitsu" ("The Fruits of Olympus"), which drew those days abroad together with the memory of a faint first love. A disciple of Dazai Osamu, he was counted among the Buraiha, and after the war he set down in his work the ruined life of joining and leaving the Communist Party and of sinking into drink and sleeping pills. In 1949 he cut his wrists before Dazai's grave at Zenrin-ji temple in Mitaka, taking his own life at the age of 36.
