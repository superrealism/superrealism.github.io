Biography

Born in 1931 into the Kawasaki zaibatsu family of Kobe; her real name was Kawasaki Sumiko. After Kobe College, an encounter with Shimao Toshio led her to join the coterie magazine "VIKING," where she began writing under the pen name Kusaka Yōko. At eighteen, her story "Domino no Otsuge" ("The Domino's Message") became a candidate for the Akutagawa Prize, drawing attention as a precocious talent. She continued to publish stories amid unruly behavior and an intense self-consciousness, but on New Year's Eve, 1952, she threw herself in front of a train at Hankyū Rokkō Station and ended her own life at the age of 21. Her last work, "Kusaka Yōko no Tanjō to Shibō" ("The Birth and Death of Kusaka Yōko"), is a strange autobiography narrating the life and death of her own pen name.
