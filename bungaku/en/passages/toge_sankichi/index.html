Poems of the Atomic Bomb

Give me back my father, give me back my mother Give me back the old people Give me back the children Give me back myself, give back all the people bound to me As long as there is a world of human beings, a peace that will not crumble give back that peace

The “Preface” the bombed poet placed at the head of his collection. In a short appeal written entirely in plain hiragana, it demands that everything taken be given back.
