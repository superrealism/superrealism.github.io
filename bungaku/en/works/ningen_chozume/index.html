About this work

A grotesque short story by Yumeno Kyūsaku. A Japanese craftsman who went to the Saint Louis exposition in America tells a terrible and bizarre souvenir of his travels — how he came within an inch of being made into sausage — in the brash speech of a downtown Tokyo man. A grotesque piece.

Summary

"You want to hear a story from my trip abroad, is that it?" — the craftsman telling the tale begins, in the brash speech of downtown Tokyo, an experience so bizarre he would rather not recall it at all. A story of how he came within an inch of being made into sausage, thanks to the world being round — as a young man, having crossed to the Saint Louis World's Fair in America, he wanders by some mischance into a terrible place underground. It turned out to be a hair-raising factory that made sausages out of human beings. Thanks to the help of a girl named Miss Fui, who had fallen for him, he escapes just before being turned into sausage himself — but later, cutting up a sausage to go with the Japanese beer he'd bought on board ship, he finds, coming out of the cut end, a black woman's hair and a small scrap of paper. Could it be that Miss Fui, who loved him, had been made into sausage in his place, and had followed him all this way...? The "round logic of the world" turns his head into a jumble, like a sausage itself. A grotesque and bizarre-tasting piece of storytelling, thoroughly characteristic of Yumeno Kyūsaku.

About the app

The opening (original text)

You want to hear a story from my trip abroad, is that it? Well now, let's see... it's such an old business I've gone and forgotten most of it. If it's all the same to you, I'd rather be let off — it's only a tale of how I got the fright of my life and came running home with my life barely intact, that's all it is... ...Eh? That story. What story would that be, you ask? Ah. The one about how the world being round, I very nearly got made into sausage myself...

Ningen chōzume by Yumeno Kyūsaku (Aozora Bunko)

The ending (original text)

Could it be that Miss Fui, who loved me, had gone and got herself made into sausage in my place, and followed me all this way...? Once a thought like that gets into your head, it's no longer any kind of thing to have with your beer, I can tell you. My own head got all jumbled up like a sausage itself. And I got to feeling I understood, more and more, that round logic of the world... truly... ah, indeed...

Ningen chōzume by Yumeno Kyūsaku (Aozora Bunko)
