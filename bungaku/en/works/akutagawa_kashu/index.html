About this work

A collection of the tanka Akutagawa wrote. Under headnotes such as "Purple Velvet," "Paulownia" and "Roses," poems of a fresh lyricism are set out. A glimpse of the poet in a man known as a novelist.

Summary

"Is it like the feel of stroking a deep purple velvet, softly — the spring going out" — a collection of the tanka Akutagawa Ryūnosuke composed from time to time. Under headings such as Purple Velvet, Paulownia, Roses, Love on a Journey, Young People, and A Long Day on the Sand, there are poems on the turning of the seasons, on the mood of travel, on a faint love, on scenes of nature. Especially memorable is the sequence in which he sings again and again of the beach sedge growing in the sand by the sea, together with the passing of the shadows of clouds: "When the shadow of a cloud falls, at once, deeply, the beach sedge lies down blue." Fresh and clear poems, giving a glimpse of Akutagawa — famous as a writer of fiction — in his other aspect, as a delicate and richly lyrical poet.
