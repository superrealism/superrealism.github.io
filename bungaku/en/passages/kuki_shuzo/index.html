Iki no kōzō

Finally, may we not define iki — this phenomenon of consciousness so rich in distinctive color, iki as a coquetry that realizes its own existence through ideality and unreality — as “refined (resignation), with spirit (pride), and alluring (coquetry)”?

The definition offered after analyzing iki, the aesthetic sense of Edo, into three moments: coquetry, pride, and resignation. It is celebrated as an attempt to grasp a distinctively Japanese aesthetic sensibility in the language of philosophy.
