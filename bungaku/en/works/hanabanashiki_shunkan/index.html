About this work

Written in 1952, the year Kusaka Yōko died. Nanbara Sugiko, a woman who discloses neither her age nor her past, and Anan, who weeps and runs about inside her — the form of writing one woman under two names is what makes this novel peculiar. A woman who lives behind a mask is called by that name just once. Saying that she has lived only for that instant, the story comes down.

Summary

Nanbara Sugiko appeared suddenly over two married couples. Neither her age nor her history is known. On the third day after escaping from Tokyo she became a consultant to the advertising department of a spinning company; three times a week she is the dancing teacher "Professor Akabane"; somewhere else she has ten pupils on the piano. No one knows what is true of her.

At the café Kalevala in Osaka, Sugiko is introduced to Nishina Rokurō of the broadcasting station and to Hōrai Kazuko, the mistress of the café, who sings as an amateur. Kazuko was Rokurō's lover ten years ago, and her husband Hōrai Kensuke is in time drawn to Sugiko. And between Rokurō and Sugiko a love begins that is established in silence, without a word exchanged. In his heart Rokurō calls her "Anan." They have never told each other that they love, nor spoken of the future. Only, he has a wife, Takako, and Anan has no brake.

Kazuko's jealousy, Kensuke's approaches, and then the appearance of Rokurō's wife. Before Sugiko, who has meant to cut the overlapping triangles and leave only the line between Rokurō and Anan, the fair, slender-faced Takako appears. At an evening gathering where all five are together, Sugiko tells as an invented story "a vow once offered to a man's death," makes Kazuko sing, and then turns to the piano herself. When the improvised variations stopped, a voice rose calling that name.

Characters

  • Nanbara Sugiko (Anan)　A woman who tells neither her age nor her history. She has an outward name and an inward one
  • Nishina Rokurō　A married man working at a broadcasting company, who calls her "Anan"
  • Nishina Takako　Rokurō's wife, who trusts her husband entirely
  • Hōrai Kazuko　The mistress of the café Kalevala and Rokurō's former lover, who sings as an amateur
  • Hōrai Kensuke　Kazuko's husband, who is drawn to Sugiko

The opening (original text)

Nanbara Sugiko. Born under the day of Mars. Under the influence of the war god Mars, nearest to the earth, she radiates that energy most intensely. She has a fighting nature and is positive toward her object, but her fate is full of trouble. Moreover, at the end of her life she stands at a complex crossing and ignores the signal, and so brings calamity on herself. Nanbara Sugiko appeared suddenly over a small society — that is, over two married couples. Neither her age nor her history is known.

The ending (original text)

"What is the matter with you?" Takako sent him a glance as uneasy as his own. Nishina Rokurō sat down dejectedly in his chair. At that moment Hōrai Kazuko drained the glass beside her at a draft and gave a hysterical laugh. — Anan cannot go on living. In all her life there will never be such a great happiness, such a brilliant moment, again. "Anan" — and your voice. Anan's moment of happiness, her brilliant moment —
