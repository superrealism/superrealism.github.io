Biography

Born in 1902 in Hakodate, Hokkaido; his real name was Abe Masao. After working as a newspaper reporter he went to France and studied under the director Charles Dullin. On returning to Japan he worked in theater direction while also beginning to write fiction for the magazine "Shinseinen," and was known as "a magician of the novel" for a versatile style that moved freely between detective fiction, historical fiction, and contemporary stories. He is remembered both for popular serialized novels such as "Machito" ("The Phantom City"), "Kin Rō" ("The Golden Wolf"), and the "Agojūrō Torimonochō" detective series, and for meticulously crafted short stories such as "Prophecy," "Mother and Child," and "Hamlet." He died in 1957.
