About this work

A man watching, with a quiet gaze, a friend who has done something irreparable while drunk — that opening scene fixes the core of the story. Kinnosuke, who shields his friend and withdraws without ever speaking of what he feels, mutters again and again afterwards that he should have said it was wrong. The sound of plum blossom falling in the rain binds one day in the past to the present, in a short story that leaves a quiet afterglow.

Summary

"I am sorry; I never meant it so; if only I had not been drunk — but there is nothing to be done" — so Hanzaburō said, and hung his head. A pale face that seems to declare an unhealthy life, slack loose lips, clouded eyes that will not fix. Kinnosuke looked at his friend with quiet eyes. His own face, with its heavy brows and firm mouth, which gives rather a keen impression, holds now, as he looks at his friend, a mild and warm color — the story begins from this contrast. ... Kinnosuke had a woman in his heart: Yurie. But the match with Kimisato goes forward, and Yurie tells him, "If I go without taking the mirror with me, something will be left behind in my heart." What that declared was only too plain. That she should have come herself, not through anyone else, to tell him how things stood — was that not proof of a close unspoken understanding between them? And thinking so, Kinnosuke said nothing. "I should have said that it would not do," he mutters again and again to himself; "I was not honest; if Yurie is to be unhappy because of this, it is all the fault of my own thoughtlessness." In time he makes up his mind to see Kimisato, and asks Yurie to tell him to be at home. At the end of the story, when Yurie's retreating figure has gone out of sight, there is suddenly, on his right, the sound of something falling with a thud. A plum has fallen — and turning at the thought, Kinnosuke sees the trees stripped bare by the sleet, realizes his mistake, and murmurs with a wry smile: "Yes.... It was raining that time too."

Characters

  • Kinnosuke　The man who shields his friend and never speaks his own feeling
  • Hanzaburō　The friend who has done, in drink, what cannot be undone
  • Yurie　The woman Kinnosuke keeps in his heart
  • Kimisato　The man to whom Yurie is matched
