Sugawa Kunihiko: Life and Major Works

Life
1880–1949
Wikipedia
Wikipedia

Biography

Born in 1880. He graduated from the Tokyo Higher Mercantile Marine School and served as captain on international routes before returning to his alma mater as a professor to train young men for the sea. During his time as a student he had heard, from his instructor Nakagawa Kurakichi, the story of the wreck of the sailing ship Ryūsui-maru and the ordeal of its sixteen crewmen surviving on a deserted island; forty-six years later he wrote it up as a book for young readers, "Mujintō ni Ikiru Jūrokunin" ("Sixteen Men Who Lived on a Deserted Island"). The true story of men who survived a desperate situation through discipline, ingenuity, and good cheer has gone through many printings ever since. He died in 1949.
