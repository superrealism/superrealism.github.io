Hashimoto Gorō: Life and Major Works

Life
1903–1948

Biography

Born in 1903. A writer of detective fiction in the early Shōwa period. He published in coterie magazines such as "Tantei Shumi" ("Detective Taste") and "Shinseinen" ("New Youth"), and Edogawa Ranpo praised him as "a writer with the temperament of a poet." Known for short stories combining bizarre conceits with a lyrical narrative voice, his best-known work, "The Story of a Man Who Bought a Suicide," is a poignant tale about a young painter who advertises in the newspaper that he wishes to "buy a suicide." Though he left few works, he continues to be reassessed today as a writer for the now-legendary detective magazines. He died in 1948 at the age of 45.
