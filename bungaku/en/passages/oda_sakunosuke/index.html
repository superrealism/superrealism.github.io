Sweet Beans for Two

“D-do you know why th-this place always brings the sweet bean soup in t-two bowls? Bet you don't. See, this shop was started way back by some jōruri master or other, and he figured two small bowls looks like more than one big one piled high — that was his clever idea.” Chōko said, “I'd say it means two together are better than one alone.”

The closing exchange at the “Meoto Zenzai” (“Sweet Bean Soup for Two”) shop by Hōzenji Temple. Reading the shape of a marriage into the reason a single bowl is served as two, this is the celebrated ending of a novel of ordinary Osaka life.
