The Five-Storied Pagoda

Seated alone before a long, sturdily built brazier of fine-grained zelkova wood, its rim deliberately fitted with red oak, with no one to talk to, sat a woman of about thirty, looking somewhat forlorn.

An opening that builds outward from the fine detail of a woman before a long brazier. In Rohan's characteristically elegant style, it opens the story of the carpenter Nosso Jūbei, who stakes his whole life on a single pagoda.
