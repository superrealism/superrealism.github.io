Biography

Born in 1880 in Misato Village, Nagaoka District, Kōchi Prefecture (now Kōchi City); his art names were Tōyō and Kōdarō. Self-taught, he made his way into letters, going up to Tokyo to work as a newspaper reporter and a writer of biographies. He is known for the work of ranging over Chinese tales of the strange and over the accounts of the uncanny handed down in every part of Japan, retelling them in a modern voice and gathering them into "Nihon Kaidan Zenshū" ("Complete Ghost Stories of Japan"). He was the driving force of modern ghost-story literature, devoting his life to the collecting and retelling of such tales. He died in 1941 at the age of 60.
