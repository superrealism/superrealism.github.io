Summer Flowers

Glittering fragments ash-white cinders spread wide like a panorama the strange rhythm of human corpses, burnt red and festering Did all this happen, or was it only something that could happen? The world after being torn away in a flash Beside an overturned streetcar the way the horses' bellies swell the smell of smoldering electric wire

The katakana poem inserted in the scene of riding by horse cart through Hiroshima just after the atomic bombing. True to the author's remark that “the impressions of this area seem better scrawled in katakana,” it sets down a scene that seems beyond reality in dry, angular katakana.
