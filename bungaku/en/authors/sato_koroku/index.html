Biography

Born in 1874 into a former samurai family of Hirosaki, Aomori Prefecture; his real name was Kōroku, written with different characters. He went up to Tokyo to study haiku under Masaoka Shiki, was active as a newspaper reporter and a playwright, and then turned to fiction. "Aa Gyokuhai ni Hana Ukete" ("Ah, Flowers Float in the Jeweled Cup"), serialized in "Shōnen Kurabu" from 1927, was an explosive success, and with the series of works that followed he became the foremost writer of boys' fiction. The poet Satō Hachirō and the novelist Satō Aiko are his children. He died in 1949 at the age of 74.
