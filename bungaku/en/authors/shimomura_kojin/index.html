Biography

Born in 1884 in Kanzaki District, Saga Prefecture; his real name was Torokurō. After graduating in English literature from Tokyo Imperial University, he served for many years as an educator, holding posts including principal of middle schools in Saga and Taiwan and principal of Taihoku Higher School. After retiring he threw himself into the youth-association movement, and alongside that work continued to write the autobiographical epic novel "Jirō Monogatari" ("The Story of Jirō"), which became his signature work of boys' fiction. He also devoted himself to bringing the classics of the East to modern readers in accessible language, retelling the figures of Confucius and his disciples as narrative in "Rongo Monogatari" ("The Story of the Analects") and rendering the entire text into plain modern Japanese in "Gendaiyaku Rongo" ("The Analects in Modern Translation"). He died in 1955 at the age of 70.
