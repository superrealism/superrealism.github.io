Biography

Born in 1896 in Odawara, Kanagawa Prefecture. He studied English literature at Waseda University. Starting out with I-novel-like works, he later established a distinctive style all his own — fantastical and comic, likening the landscape of his hometown to Greek mythology — earning him the nickname "Greek Makino." With works such as "Zeron" and "Mura no Sutoaha" ("The Village Stoics"), he stood out even among the writers of the New Art School, and had a strong influence on younger writers, including Sakaguchi Ango. Tormented by neurasthenia, he took his own life at his family home in Odawara in 1936.
