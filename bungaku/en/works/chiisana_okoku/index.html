About this work

A short story Tanizaki published in Chūgai in 1918. A boy called Numakura transfers into the class of Kaijima Shōkichi, an elementary teacher struggling to make ends meet. Numakura has an uncanny power to lead, and before long he has the children following him and has built a small kingdom that even issues its own currency. An unusual work in which Tanizaki draws, in cold psychological detail, the uncanny power a children's game can take on and the poor teacher who is swallowed by it.

Summary

Kaijima Shōkichi is a true son of Edo, yet his love of scholarship has left him a failure at making his way in the world, and he ekes out a hard living as an elementary school teacher. Burdened with a poor household soon to welcome a seventh child, he transfers to an elementary school in the provincial city of M, and into the classroom he takes charge of transfers a boy named Numakura. Unremarkable in appearance, Numakura nonetheless possesses an uncanny gift for command, and before long the children of the class come to look up to him as their chieftain and follow him without question. Under Numakura, the children set up a government of their own, divide roles among themselves, and at last build a "small kingdom" complete with its own currency, valid only among themselves. Kaijima, who had at first dismissed it all as mere child's play, finds himself drawn in, little by little, by the sheer perfection of their organization and by the uncanny dignity that clings to Numakura. In the depths of his poverty, Kaijima one day finds himself unable even to raise money to buy medicine for his sick child, and at last accepts the banknotes issued by Numakura's "kingdom" as though they were real money. A singular piece in which Tanizaki, with a cold and unflinching pen, depicts the uncanny power that lurks within children's play, and the poor teacher who is gradually swallowed up by it.

Characters

  • Kaijima Shōkichi　An elementary school teacher who ekes out a hard living. A true son of Edo. Gradually swallowed up by the "small kingdom" Numakura builds
  • Numakura　A boy who transfers into Kaijima's classroom. Commands the class with an uncanny gift for leadership, and builds a "kingdom" complete with its own currency

The opening (original text)

It was some two years ago now, when he was just thirty-six, that Kaijima Shōkichi transferred to the elementary school in the city of M, in G Prefecture. He was a true son of Edo, born in the Shōden-chō district of Asakusa, but whether from some inherited trait of his father, a scholar of Chinese learning in the days of the old shogunate, he had loved scholarship since earliest childhood, and it was this, in the end, that had led him to misspend his whole life — or so, at least, he now thought, resigned to it.
