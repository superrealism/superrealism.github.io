About this work

A Boy Detectives story Ranpo serialized in Shōnen Kurabu in 1958. The story begins when the businessman Kamiyama Masao is threatened by the Fiend with Forty Faces. In a strange castle of masks, the great detective Akechi Kogorō, the Boy Detectives and the boys of the irregulars face the fiend. A favourite entry in the series.

Summary

One day, a worried-looking Kamiyama Masao, a businessman living in Minato Ward, Tokyo, comes to call at Akechi's detective office in Kōjimachi. Kamiyama, a director of many companies, brings a letter of introduction from a close friend of Akechi's and makes a fearful confession: "I am being blackmailed by the Fiend with Forty Faces." So begins the strange affair surrounding the master of disguise, the Fiend with Forty Faces. The setting is the bizarre "Castle of Strange Masks." The great detective Akechi Kogorō, the Boy Detectives Club led by young Kobayashi, and the "Ragtag Squad" made up of boys who were once street urchins, join forces to close in on the fiend and his disguises and schemes. After one crisis after another, the boys at last triumphantly defeat the fiend. "Hurrah for Mr. Akechi! Hurrah for Captain Kobayashi!" — to the boys crying out with tears in their eyes, young Kobayashi answers, tears in his own eyes as well, "Hurrah for the Boy Detectives Club, hurrah for the Ragtag Squad!" An entry in the series that stirs the heart with the boys' exploits.

The opening (original text)

One day, a distinguished-looking gentleman came calling at Akechi's detective office in a high-class apartment building in Kōjimachi. He was a businessman named Kamiyama Masao, who lived in Minato Ward, Tokyo, and served as a director of many companies. Mr. Kamiyama had come bearing a letter of introduction from a businessman friend close to Detective Akechi.

Kimenjō no himitsu by Edogawa Ranpo (Aozora Bunko)

The ending (original text)

"Hurrah for Mr. Akechi! Hurrah for Captain Kobayashi!" At this, young Kobayashi too, tears welling in his eyes, cried out in answer. "Hurrah for the Boy Detectives Club, hurrah for the Ragtag Squad!"
