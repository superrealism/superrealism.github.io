About this work

Shimomura Kojin's major work and a classic of the Japanese novel of formation. Honda Jirō, put out to a wet nurse soon after birth, is torn between her and his real mother, and through the decline of his family and his mother's death grows from boy to young man. Written in five parts, it was left unfinished partway through the fifth by the author's death. The thought of a man who gave his life to the education of the young runs through the whole, and the theme of forming a person — caught in phrases such as "the plan of having no plan" and "digging into oneself" — is carried through the fine detail of a boy's days. This app carries all five parts.

Summary

Characters

  • Honda Jirō　A boy sent out to a wet nurse and raised torn between her and his own mother
  • O-Hama　The wet nurse who brought Jirō up, and the stay of his heart
  • Honda O-Tami　Jirō's mother, who keeps him at a hard distance and is reconciled with him on her sickbed
  • Honda Shunryō　Jirō's father, a large-hearted and warm-natured man
  • Honda Kyōichi　Jirō's elder brother
  • Honda Shunzō　Jirō's younger brother
  • The old man and woman at Masaki　O-Tami's parents, who take Jirō in warmly
  • Mr. Asakura　Jirō's middle school teacher, who guides the Swan Society and shapes the course of his life
  • Michie　A girl for whom Jirō has feelings
  • Ōkawa Mumon　A silent student whom Jirō meets at the Yūai School
