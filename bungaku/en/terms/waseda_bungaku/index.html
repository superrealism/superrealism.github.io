What is Waseda Bungaku?

Category
Society / Group

A literary magazine founded in 1891 by Tsubouchi Shōyō, who rallied students of the literature department at Tokyo Semmon Gakkō (now Waseda University). At first it took a form close to a lecture record, but from the reorganization of its pages in September 1893 it became the venue for Shōyō's own critical writing and functioned as the theoretical stronghold of realism. In this first period it also became one of the stages for the debate between Shōyō and Mori Ōgai over the ideals of literature. From its second incarnation, beginning in 1906, Shimamura Hōgetsu took the lead, and, as the venue for publishing the Naturalist literary movement, it led the literary establishment. As a pioneer among university-affiliated literary magazines representing the Waseda lineage, as against Keio University's "Mita Bungaku," it has carved out a long history through repeated hiatuses and revivals.
