Biography

Born in 1899 to a shipping agent's family in Mikawa Town, Ishikawa District, Ishikawa Prefecture (now Hakusan City). He lost his father early and grew up in poverty. "Chijō" ("On Earth"), the long novel he published in 1919 at the age of twenty, became one of the great bestsellers of the Taishō era and made him overnight the darling of his day. But his arrogant claims to "genius" isolated him from literary circles, and an incident involving a woman destroyed him socially. He later fell mentally ill, was committed to a hospital, and died in 1930 at the age of 31.
