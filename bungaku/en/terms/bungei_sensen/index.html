What is Bungei Sensen?

Category
Society / Group

A proletarian literature magazine founded in June 1924 when the coterie members of "Tanemaku Hito" ("The Sower"), which had ceased publication amid the chaos of the Great Kantō Earthquake, regrouped. Centered on Komaki Ōmi and Aono Suekichi, it soon became the central organ of the proletarian literature movement. Hayama Yoshiki published "Inbaifu" ("The Prostitute," 1925) and "Letter Found in a Cement Barrel" (1926) here, establishing himself as a leading writer of early proletarian literature. As the movement's organization repeatedly fractured, the magazine became, from 1927 on, the organ of the social-democratic Rōnō Geijutsuka Renmei (League of Proletarian Artists), standing in sharp opposition to the Communist-aligned NAPF's organ "Senki" ("The Battle Flag") and carrying one of the two great currents of the proletarian literature movement. Amid suppression and the movement's decline, it ceased publication in July 1932.
