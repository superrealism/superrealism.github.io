What is Fujin Kōron?

Category
Society / Group

A general-interest magazine for women founded in January 1916 by the Chūō Kōron publishing company. Building on the success of a special feature on women's issues in the parent magazine "Chūō Kōron," it launched under founding editor Shimanaka Yūsaku with progressive pages promoting women's liberation and independence. Advocates of the "New Woman" such as Hiratsuka Raichō and Yosano Akiko wrote vigorously for it, and the celebrated "motherhood protection debate" the two waged in its pages from 1918 into the following year — over whether the state should guarantee maternal welfare or women should prioritize economic independence — is renowned as a debate primarily staged in this magazine. Aimed chiefly at educated women readers, it discussed women's issues and the home while also devoting energy to literature, publishing many works that reflected the times: Arishima Takeo's "Oborekaketa Kyōdai" ("The Nearly Drowned Brother and Sister"), Akutagawa Ryūnosuke's "Saru Kani Gassen" ("The Battle of the Monkey and the Crab") and "Shinkirō" ("Mirage"), the latter half of Tanizaki Jun'ichirō's "Sasameyuki" ("The Makioka Sisters"), Dazai Osamu's "Jūnigatsu Yōka" ("December Eighth"), and Sakaguchi Ango's "Ren'airon" ("On Love").
