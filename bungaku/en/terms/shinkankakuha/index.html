What is Shinkankakuha (New Sensationist School)?

Category
Literary Movement

An avant-garde group that sought to renovate established realism, centered on the coterie magazine "Bungei Jidai," founded by Yokomitsu Riichi and Kawabata Yasunari in 1924 after the Great Kantō Earthquake. The critic Chiba Kameo coined the name. Yokomitsu put forward a style rich in sensory metaphor and bold personification, and made an impression for this group's direction with "Atama Narabi ni Hara" ("Heads and Bellies," 1924), opening with the line "It was noon. The special express train ran full speed ahead, packed with passengers." In his later work "Kikai" ("Machine," 1930) he pursued experimental psychological depiction incorporating the stream of consciousness. It stood opposed, in a rivalry over whether sensibility or ideology should take priority in literature, to the "Bungei Sensen" faction of proletarian literature that rose to prominence in the same period, but as an organized movement it splintered and dispersed within a few years, and Yokomitsu and Kawabata each moved on to their own distinctive styles.
