What is Senki (The Battle Flag)?

Category
Society / Group

A proletarian literature magazine founded in May 1928 as the organ of the All-Japan Federation of Proletarian Arts (NAPF), formed by the merger of the Japan Proletarian Arts League and the Vanguard Artists' League. Kobayashi Takiji published "1928-nen 3-gatsu 15-nichi" ("March 15, 1928," 1928), taking as its subject the authorities' suppression in the Three-Fifteen Incident, and his representative work "Kanikōsen" ("The Crab Cannery Ship," 1929), depicting a mass of laborers driven to exhaustion in the crab-canning fishery, in this magazine, sending landmark works of proletarian literature into the world. Tokunaga Sunao's "Taiyō no Nai Machi" ("The Street Without Sun") was likewise a much-discussed work in the magazine. Amid mounting government suppression, it ceased publication in December 1931 after 41 issues in all, and thereafter the proletarian literature movement itself declined rapidly.
