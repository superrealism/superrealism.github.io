What is Subaru?

Category
Society / Group

A literary magazine founded in January 1909, taking up the mantle after "Myōjō" ceased publication the previous November. It was published with the backing of Mori Ōgai and the husband-and-wife tanka poets Yosano Kan (Tekkan) and Akiko, and Ishikawa Takuboku served as the publisher of record for the inaugural issue. Coterie members from the "Myōjō" era, including Kinoshita Mokutarō, Takamura Kōtarō, Kitahara Hakushū, and Yoshii Isamu, gathered here, developing an anti-Naturalist, Romantic style that came to be called the "Subaru school." Ōgai published "Vita Sexualis" (1909, banned from sale) and "Gan" ("The Wild Goose," 1911–13) in this magazine, sending one work at the height of his powers into the world after another. It ceased publication in December 1913 after 60 issues in all.
