What is All Yomimono?

Category
Society / Group

A popular fiction magazine launched by Kikuchi Kan's Bungei Shunjū publishing company in July 1930 as a special supplementary issue of "Bungei Shunjū" titled "All Yomimono Gō." Well received, it became an independent monthly in April 1931, and its inaugural issue carried the first installment of Nomura Kodō's "Zenigata Heiji Torimonokae," written at the request of an editor who wanted something in the vein of Okamoto Kidō's "Hanshichi Torimonochō." Heiji's cases went on to be written for the next twenty-seven years, running to 383 episodes, and won nationwide popularity. Whereas Akutagawa Prize–winning works are published in "Bungei Shunjū," it is customary for the judges' comments and the winning work of the Naoki Prize, established the same year, 1935, to appear in this magazine, and it has remained a central venue for popular fiction ever since. Yamamoto Shūgorō's linked stories "Akahige Shinyōtan" ("Red Beard"), depicting the bond between the doctor known as "Red Beard" at the Koishikawa charity clinic and a young intern, were also serialized here (March–December 1958 issues). The magazine continues publication to this day.
