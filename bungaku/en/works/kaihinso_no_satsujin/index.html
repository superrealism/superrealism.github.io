About this work

A detective story Yamamoto Shūgorō published in Shōnen Shōjo Tankai in 1938. A murder at the seaside villa Kaihinsō — the riddle around a family under the shadow of misfortune arrives in time at an unexpected truth. A piece memorable for an ending in which one shaft of bright light falls on a house of tragedy.

Summary

"Eru, not yet?" — Takano Sen'ichi's voice, peering shamelessly in from the veranda. Shizuko, at her dressing table applying powder against sunburn, starts: "Stop it, Sen-chan, peeking in from a place like that!" People gather at the seaside villa Kaihinsō — but a shadow of misfortune lies over this house. A mystery deepens around a murder that soon takes place. What has really happened in this house they call the "house of tragedy"? The truth of the case comes to light little by little, and the story moves toward an unexpected end. At the close, to the words "Yes — to you alone..." Sen'ichi claps his hands and cries, "I've got it!" It was the first bright voice ever to ring through this house of tragedy. A detective story of Yamamoto's letting a single shaft of hope fall into a dark case.

Characters

  • Takano Sen'ichi　A young man caught up in the case
  • Shizuko　A woman at Kaihinsō
