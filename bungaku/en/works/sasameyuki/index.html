About this work

The long novel Tanizaki wrote across the war and into the years after it. Set in the region between Osaka and Kobe in the 1930s, it follows the four daughters of the Makioka family, an old Osaka merchant house, turning on the search for a husband for the third sister Yukiko and the love affairs of the fourth, Taeko, and unrolling the vanishing upper-class culture of the Kansai and its calendar of seasonal observances like a picture scroll. Serialization began in Chūō Kōron in 1943 but was stopped under military pressure as unsuited to the times; it was completed in 1948. Widely regarded as the summation of Tanizaki's work. This app carries it in three volumes.
