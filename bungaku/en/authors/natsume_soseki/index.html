Biography

Born in Edo's Ushigome district in 1867; his real name was Natsume Kinnosuke. After graduating in English literature from Tokyo Imperial University, he taught English in Matsuyama and Kumamoto, and, following study in England, made his literary debut with "Wagahai wa Neko de Aru" ("I Am a Cat"). He left a wealth of masterpieces — "Botchan," "Sanshirō," "Sore Kara" ("And Then"), and "Kokoro," the culminating work of his late trilogy — and is celebrated as the national writer who represents modern Japanese literature. He died in 1916 of a gastric ulcer at the age of 49 while writing "Meian" ("Light and Darkness").
