About this work

A long novel whose first volume was serialized in the Yomiuri Shimbun in 1910 and whose second appeared in Chūō Kōron the following year. It draws the fall and collapse, over more than ten years, of two houses: the Koizumi family, an old house of Kiso, and the Hashimoto family related to them by marriage. It writes not one outdoor scene, keeping doggedly to the inside of the houses, and is held to be a central work of naturalism. Tōson used his own family and relations as material without disguise, and projected himself into Sankichi, the youngest son, struggling against the fate that runs in an old family's blood. Both volumes are carried here separately.

Characters

  • Koizumi Sankichi　The youngest of the Koizumi, who makes his way as a teacher and by the pen and tries to escape the fate of an old house
  • O-Yuki　Sankichi's wife, who upholds her husband and children and keeps the house
  • Hashimoto O-Tane　Sankichi's elder sister, the mistress of the Hashimoto, an old house in Kiso
  • Hashimoto Tatsuo　O-Tane's husband, the head of the declining Hashimoto house
  • Hashimoto Shōta　O-Tane's eldest son, who sickens under the burden of the house
  • Koizumi Minoru　Sankichi's eldest brother, who brings the house down by dissipation and failure in business
  • Koizumi Morihiko　Sankichi's second brother, a doctor
