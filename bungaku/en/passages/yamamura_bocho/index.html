Famous passages by Yamamura Bochō

Well-known lines from the works of Yamamura Bochō.

2 passages

Landscape

Rapeseed flowers everywhere Rapeseed flowers everywhere Rapeseed flowers everywhere Rapeseed flowers everywhere Rapeseed flowers everywhere Rapeseed flowers everywhere Rapeseed flowers everywhere a faint wheat-whistle Rapeseed flowers everywhere

An experimental poem that conjures the sheer expanse of a rapeseed field simply by tiling the same line over and over. As its subtitle, “A Mosaic of Pure Silver,” suggests, it builds a landscape purely by arranging words.

Clouds

Hey there, cloud drifting along so easy aren't you awfully carefree where are you off to all the way to Iwaki-Taira, are you?

A short poem that does nothing more than speak to a cloud in the sky. Using only plain colloquial language and the name of a place, it captures directly an unhurried, expansive state of mind — a representative late work of Bochō's.
