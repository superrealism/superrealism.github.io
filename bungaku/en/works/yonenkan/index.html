About this work

A novel Yamamoto Shūgorō published in Shin Seinen in 1946. The young Shinzō, struck by an illness hard to cure, and Masako, who supports him. It draws in a quiet hand the two of them living close together under the shadow of death.

Summary

A young doctor was at a loss over Shinzō's condition. Even after three X-ray photographs and various blood and urine tests, he could not decide in what words to deliver his diagnosis — so serious was Shinzō's illness, and so difficult to cure. From the young doctor's troubled expression, sweat beading on his brow, Shinzō quietly grasps his own fate. In the days when the shadow of death has begun to fall over him, Shinzō has a woman he cares for, named Masako. To Masako, clinging to him and saying, "Please don't leave me alone," Shinzō speaks quietly. "I've kept a bottle of champagne set aside for tonight — I thought I'd never get the chance to use it, but here it is. Let's celebrate, just the two of us." A piece of Yamamoto's, drawing in restrained prose the figures of two people still trying to live facing forward, within their limited time.

Characters

  • Shinzō　A young man afflicted with a serious illness
  • Masako　The woman who stays close by his side

The opening (original text)

"How about here — does this hurt?" The doctor spoke as he quietly moved the probe. "No pain there either, I see... and here?" Shinzō watched the doctor's face. He still looked young, with little clinical experience; whenever he came up against a case that was hard to cure, it could not help but show in his expression. Even now sweat was beading on his brow, and however casual he tried to appear, he could not fully hide the color of his confusion.

Yonenkan by Yamamoto Shūgorō (Aozora Bunko)

The ending (original text)

"...Please don't leave me alone." "I'm only going down to the cellar." Shinzō said this, looking steadily at Masako. "...I've kept a bottle of champagne set aside for tonight — I thought I'd never get the chance to use it, but here it is. Let's celebrate, just the two of us. I'll be right back."
