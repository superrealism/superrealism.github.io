Biography

Born in Komatsu, Ishikawa Prefecture, in 1901. He went from the Fourth Higher School to graduate in English literature from Tokyo Imperial University in 1925. Active as a leading prewar translator of English and American literature, he translated "The Complete Tales of Edgar Allan Poe" for the Daiichi Shobō publishing house in 1931-1932, drawing attention as a major figure in introducing Poe properly to Japanese readers. He also left many still-read translations, including Stevenson's "Treasure Island" and "The Strange Case of Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde" and Dickens's "A Tale of Two Cities." His lucid, readable prose has been carried forward into postwar paperback editions. He died suddenly of pneumonia in 1943 at the age of 42.
