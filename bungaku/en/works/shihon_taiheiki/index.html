About this work

Yoshikawa Eiji's last great work, serialized in the Mainichi Shimbun for more than three years from 1958. Drawing on the Taiheiki but placing at its centre Ashikaga Takauji, who had always been told of as a traitor, it recasts the upheaval from the fall of the Kamakura shogunate through the collapse of the Kenmu Restoration to the split between the two courts as the inner struggle of one commander — hence "my own" Taiheiki. Emperor Go-Daigo, Kusunoki Masashige, Nitta Yoshisada and Sasaki Dōyo move among one another, and the real protagonist is the age itself, an old order coming down with a crash. This app carries all thirteen volumes.

Summary

Characters

  • Ashikaga Takauji　The head of the great Ashikaga house of the Minamoto line, who serves Kamakura and then steps out into the turning of the age
  • Ashikaga Tadayoshi　Takauji's younger brother, who upholds him and later parts from him
  • Emperor Go-Daigo　The sovereign who holds the ideal of personal rule and plots the overthrow of the shogunate
  • Kusunoki Masashige　A local magnate of Kawachi, who plays with the shogunate's great armies with a handful of men
  • Nitta Yoshisada　A vassal in Kōzuke, who takes Kamakura
  • Hōjō Takatoki　The last regent of the Kamakura shogunate, given over to dengaku and dog fighting
  • Sasaki Dōyo　A basara lord who reads which way the times are running and swims between the two sides
  • Hino Toshimoto　A close attendant of Go-Daigo, who works for the plot to overthrow the shogunate
  • Prince Morinaga　Go-Daigo's son, known as Ōtō-no-miya, who comes to an ill-starred end
  • Kō no Moronao　Steward of the Ashikaga house, a man of force and power
