Biography

Born in Tokyo in 1919. Her father, Yamazaki Haruhiro, founded Japan's first beauty school. After Ochanomizu Girls' High School she trained as a beautician, and after losing her husband to the war, she lived and worked as a beautician in Mitaka. In 1947 she met Dazai Osamu and became deeply devoted to him, nursing and supporting him through his final year. On June 13, 1948, she drowned herself with Dazai in the Tamagawa Canal, dying at the age of 28. Her diary of those days, "Ame no Tamagawa Shinjū" ("Love Suicide in the Tamagawa Rain"), is known as a record kept at the closest possible remove of the last year of Dazai's life.
