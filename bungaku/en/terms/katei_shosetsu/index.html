What is Katei Shōsetsu (Domestic Fiction)?

Category
Form / Genre

A term for fiction serialized in newspapers from the 1900s onward and read by a broad readership, taking as its theme the subtleties of feeling within the home and the suffering of women. It is said to have spread as a reaction against the dark side of society depicted by the shinkoku shōsetsu (serious novel) and the kannen shōsetsu (ideas novel), and it often ends with a woman who has suffered under the household system being saved through love or morality. Its representative work is Tokutomi Roka's "Hototogisu" ("The Cuckoo"), serialized in the "Kokumin Shimbun" from November 1898 to the following May, depicting the tragedy of a wife, Namiko, suffering from pulmonary tuberculosis, who is forcibly torn from her husband by a mother-in-law obsessed with the family's reputation; it became one of the greatest bestsellers of the Meiji era. Ozaki Kōyō's "Konjiki Yasha" ("The Golden Demon"), serialized in the "Yomiuri Shimbun" at around the same time, is likewise sometimes said to share the character of domestic fiction, as a work depicting the tragedy of the home.
