About this work

A set of linked stories serialized in the magazine All Yomimono in 1958. At the Koishikawa dispensary for the poor of Edo, Yasumoto Noboru, a young and arrogant physician just back from Nagasaki, works under the rough and severe head doctor Niide Kyojō, called Red Beard, and — facing the lives and deaths of poor patients — learns what medicine is really for and what a human being is worth. Around Red Beard's severity and tenderness, and his insistence that poverty rather than disease is the root of the trouble, it draws the joys and griefs of ordinary people. The source for Kurosawa Akira's film Red Beard. This app carries all eight stories.
