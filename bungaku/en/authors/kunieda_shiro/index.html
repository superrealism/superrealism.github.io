Biography

Born in 1887 in Miyagawa Village, Suwa District, Nagano Prefecture (now Chino City). He graduated from the English literature department of Waseda University and at first wrote plays, but later won popularity publishing tales of the fantastic and the supernatural in magazines such as "Kōdan Kurabu" and "Shinseinen." With works like "Shinshū Kōketsujō" ("The Crimson Castle of the Divine Land"), "Tsutakazura Kiso no Kakehashi" ("Ivy Vines on the Kiso Trestle"), and "Yatsugatake no Majin" ("The Demon of Yatsugatake"), he built a gorgeous narrative world in which the weird, the fantastic, and the decadent were dissolved together, and is called the summit of the genre. He is known too for leaving many works unfinished because of the sheer scale of his conceptions, and even that collapse was praised as part of his appeal. He died in 1943 at the age of 55.
