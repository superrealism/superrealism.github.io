Well-known lines from the works of Tokutomi Roka.

2 passages

Nami-ko

Oh, it's hard! It's so hard! Never — never again will I be born a woman!

The anguished cry of Namiko, torn apart by an incurable illness, near the moment of her death. This celebrated scene from a bestseller moved all of Meiji Japan to tears.

Ah, why must people die! I want to live! I want to live a thousand years, ten thousand years! If we die, let it be the two of us together! Please, the two of us!

The words Namiko, ill with tuberculosis, speaks to her husband Takeo by the sea at Zushi. Her desperate cry of longing to live is known as a famous scene of this novel, which drew tears from readers of the Meiji era.
