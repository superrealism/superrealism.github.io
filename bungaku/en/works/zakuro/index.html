About this work

A historical story Yamamoto Shūgorō published in the Sun Shashin Shinbun in 1948. A marriage broken because, being young, she could not understand her husband Shōzō. After many years, the aged Masa passes quiet last days with an old man called Isuke, who may be her dead husband. A fine short story drawing a woman who comes in old age to understand a truth deep in the human heart.

Summary

Masa had not disliked her husband from the very start. Nor could the failure of her marriage really be laid to the blame of her husband, Shōzō. The misfortune lay in Shōzō's own sad nature, and in the youth of Masa, who could not understand that nature. The Matsumuro house had fallen, over two generations, from the rank of middle elder down to that of common foot-soldier, and this circumstance cast a dark shadow over Shōzō's heart. Masa, seventeen and far more naive than her years, was shaken by the shock of the new life she found herself in at her husband's house, and wore herself down amid fear and pain. Grasping her hands in both of his, Shōzō would ask, "Masa, do you hate me?" — and Masa could never answer his single-minded, fierce love. In time the two of them broke apart. Many long years passed after that. At the close of the story, the aged Masa is sixty-three. She spends her quiet last days with an old man named Isuke, who may perhaps be her dead husband Shōzō. Isuke denies that he is Shōzō — but that denial could also be taken as an affirmation, in the very shape of a denial. Masa has, by now, reached the age at which one can understand a truth hidden deep within the human heart, beyond mere words and forms. After Isuke dies at Masa's side, Masa, standing before the grave marker in a snow-covered garden, is lost in thought. "...You let me spend such a good last stretch of life." Those words, whispered by Isuke at the very end, were enough to console Masa. A fine short story of Yamamoto's, drawing, with a deep, lingering resonance, the memory of a love met again in old age.

Characters

  • Masa　A woman whose marriage fails while young, who meets Isuke again in old age
  • Shōzō　Masa's husband. A man of sad nature
  • Isuke　An old man who appears in Masa's later years
