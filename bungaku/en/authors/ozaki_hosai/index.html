Biography

Born in Tottori City, Tottori Prefecture, in 1885; his real name was Hideo. He went from the First Higher School to the Faculty of Law at Tokyo Imperial University, and after graduating worked for an insurance company, rising to branch manager, but drink cost him both his career and his family. Giving up everything, he entered Kyoto's Ittōen community, and thereafter lived a life of utter poverty as the caretaker of various temples around the country. He had studied under Ogiwara Seisensui, whom he met at the First Higher School, and composed free-verse haiku unconstrained by seasonal words or the traditional 5-7-5 form. He is known for lines pared down to their absolute limit that capture solitude in a single line, such as "Seki o shite mo hitori" ("Even coughing, alone") and "Haka no ura ni mawaru" ("Coming around behind the grave"). He ended his life at the age of 41 in 1926 at the Nangōan hermitage on Shōdoshima.
