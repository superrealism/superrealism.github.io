Muraoka Hiroshi: Life and Major Works

Life
1895–1946

Biography

A scholar of English literature and translator, born in 1895. He rendered into Japanese "The Book of Tea," which Okakura Tenshin had written in English, publishing it as "Cha no Hon" through Iwanami Bunko in 1929. His translation, which preserved the dignity of the original while rendering it into readable Japanese, went on to become the most widely read standard version and served to bring Tenshin's thought to Japanese readers. He died in 1946 at the age of 51.
