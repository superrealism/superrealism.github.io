About this work

Yamakawa Heizaburō's complete translation of La Divina Commedia, the great poem written by Dante Alighieri in the early fourteenth century, made in dignified classical Japanese between the end of Meiji and the Taishō era (published 1914–1922; the base text for the Iwanami Bunko edition). Over the days around Easter of 1300, Dante, still living, is led by the Roman poet Virgil and then by Beatrice through the three realms of hell, purgatory and paradise. One of the summits of European literature, setting out the whole cosmology of medieval Christendom on a vast plan. This app carries the three books in separate volumes.
