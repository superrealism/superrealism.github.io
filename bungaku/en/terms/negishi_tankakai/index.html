What is Negishi Tanka Society?

Category
Society / Group

A tanka poetry gathering held at Shiki-an, the house in Negishi, Tokyo, where Masaoka Shiki spent his final years. Shiki, who had sharply criticized old-school waka in "A Letter to Practitioners of Tanka" (1898) and argued for a return to the "Man'yōshū," began it in March of the following year, 1899, as a regular poetry gathering, and it became a place to put into practice the movement to reform tanka on the principle of shasei (sketching from life). Oka Fumoto and Katori Hozuma gathered there, and Itō Sachio and Nagatsuka Takashi later joined as well, and the training continued around the bedridden Shiki. When Shiki died in 1902, Sachio and others carried the society forward, founding the organ magazine "Ashibi" in 1903, and that lineage crystallized into the tanka magazine "Araragi," becoming the mother body from which the Araragi school, the greatest force in modern tanka, arose.
