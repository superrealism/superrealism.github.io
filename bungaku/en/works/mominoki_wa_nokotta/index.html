About this work

The long historical novel generally held to be Yamamoto Shūgorō's finest, serialized irregularly in the Nihon Keizai Shimbun from 1954. It takes up the Date Disturbance, the succession quarrel that shook the great Sendai domain in the early Edo period. Where Harada Kai, the senior retainer, had always been drawn as a traitor who sought his lord's life, Shūgorō recasts him as a solitary man who deliberately took infamy upon himself and died to protect the domain from a shogunal plot to break it up. In a political struggle full of private interest, Kai keeps silent and explains himself to no one; his inner life is carried by the fir trees standing in his garden. Adapted as an NHK Taiga drama. This app carries all four parts.
