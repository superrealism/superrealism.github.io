Biography

The central government ministry responsible for Japan's education, academic, and cultural administration. It was established in 1871 and, in the 2001 reorganization of central government ministries, was merged with the Science and Technology Agency to become the Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology. During the postwar educational reforms, to coincide with the promulgation and enforcement of the Constitution of Japan, it compiled and published a social studies textbook for first-year middle-school students, "Atarashii Kenpō no Hanashi" ("The Story of the New Constitution," 1947). Though its use as a textbook ended after only a few years, this booklet, which explains the principles of the Constitution in plain, approachable language, has been repeatedly reprinted and read ever since as a model piece of writing introducing the Constitution.
