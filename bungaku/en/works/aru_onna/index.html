About this work

A long novel published in two parts in 1919, Arishima's finest work and the source of one of the great female figures of modern Japanese literature. Satsuki Yōko, beautiful and quick-witted, kicks through the morality of her time and tries to live true to her own passion — and is ruined by it. On the ship to America she falls violently in love with the purser Kurachi, a married man, abandons the crossing and returns to Japan; hounded by society and by the man she loves, eaten by illness, she goes toward a lonely end, traced in acute psychological detail. It grew out of "Glimpses of a Certain Woman," serialized in Shirakaba, and was heavily rewritten. This app carries the whole in two volumes.
