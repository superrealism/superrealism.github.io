Biography

Born in Okayama in 1868 and raised in Kyoto; her real name was Toyoko. She threw herself into the Freedom and People's Rights Movement, taking the platform to argue for women's rights, and later became a reporter for "Jogaku Zasshi." "Kowareta Yubiwa" ("The Broken Ring"), published in 1891, depicts in confessional form a woman who leaves her husband's house by her own will and awakens to her selfhood, and is regarded as a forerunner of modern women's literature. She wrote "Shitayuku Mizu" ("Water Flowing Below") and "Tōsei Futari Musume" ("Two Girls of Our Time"), but after her marriage she laid down her pen and withdrew into domestic life. She died in 1933 at the age of 65.
