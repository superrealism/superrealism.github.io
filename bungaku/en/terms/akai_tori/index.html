What is Akai Tori?

Category
Society / Group

A children's magazine of stories and songs, founded on July 1, 1918, by Suzuki Miekichi. Drawing on his ties to Natsume Sōseki's circle, Miekichi rallied many writers to his conviction that children deserved truly artistic reading matter, and the inaugural issue carried Akutagawa Ryūnosuke's first children's story, "The Spider's Thread" (July 1918). The magazine became the central organ of a movement in which adult writers composed original stories and songs for children, and its January 1932 issue carried "Gon Gitsune" ("Gon the Fox"), which Miekichi revised and retitled from a submission by the then-unknown young writer Niimi Nankichi — the story that launched Niimi's career. The magazine ceased publication in 1936 with Miekichi's death, but it left a lasting mark as the starting point of modern Japanese children's literature.
