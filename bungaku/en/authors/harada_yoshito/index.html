Biography

Born in Tokyo in 1918. He graduated in German literature from Tokyo Imperial University and after the war taught at the College of Arts and Sciences of the University of Tokyo. From 1954 he lectured on Japanese at the University of Hamburg, and after returning home devoted himself to the study and translation of German literature. He rendered into Japanese the major works of Franz Kafka — "The Metamorphosis," "The Castle," "The Trial" — laying the foundation of Kafka's reception in postwar Japan. He also left translations of Zweig and others. He died suddenly in 1960, just after his promotion to professor, at the age of 41.
