About this work

Kikuchi Kan's version for children of the most widely read of all the Brothers Grimm's tales (the original title is "Sneewittchen"). It is the story of a princess born with skin as white as snow, cheeks as red as blood and hair as black as ebony, and of the stepmother queen who cannot rest until her magic mirror tells her she is the fairest in the world. The three attempts on the princess's life — the lace, the poisoned comb, the poisoned apple — the mountain house of the seven dwarfs, and the glass coffin are the very models that picture books and animated films have drawn again and again ever since.

Summary

In the depth of a snowy winter, a queen sewing at an ebony window pricks her finger, sees three drops of blood fall on the snow, and wishes for a child as white as snow, as red as blood and as black as ebony. In time a princess is born exactly so and is named Snow White, but her mother the queen soon dies. The stepmother who comes after her is very beautiful but proud, and cannot pass a day without asking her magic mirror who is the fairest in the world. One day, when the princess is seven, the mirror answers that Snow White is fairer. Beside herself, the queen orders a huntsman to kill her; but the huntsman lets her go into the forest and brings back the organs of a beast in her stead. Wandering, the princess comes to a little house deep in the mountains and is taken in by seven dwarfs. But the mirror again tells the queen that she lives, and three times the queen comes disguised as a pedlar. She stops her breath with a lace, strikes her down with a poisoned comb, and at last has her eat half a poisoned apple. The dwarfs' care is of no use, and the princess is laid in a coffin of glass. A prince who passes by asks for her, and on the way, as the coffin is carried, the piece of poisoned apple is jolted from her throat —

Characters

  • Snow White　The princess with skin as white as snow. Her stepmother the queen attempts her life three times
  • The queen　Snow White's stepmother, who must have her mirror guarantee that she is the fairest in the world
  • The magic mirror　The mirror that answers, when asked, who is fairest in the world
  • The seven dwarfs　The miners who shelter Snow White in the mountains
  • The prince　The young man who falls in love with Snow White asleep in her glass coffin

The opening (original text)

Once upon a time, in the middle of winter, when the snowflakes were falling like feathers from the sky, a queen sat sewing at a window that had a frame of black ebony. And as she sewed, she looked up at the snow, and pricked her finger with the needle, so that three drops of blood fell upon the snow. And because the red looked so beautiful in the white snow, she thought to herself: "If only I had a child as white as snow, with cheeks as red as blood, and hair as black as the wood of this ebony frame."

Snow White by Kikuchi Kan (Aozora Bunko)

The ending (original text)

The queen went into the palace to which she had been invited; and when she looked, the young woman who was to be queen was none other than Snow White. She stood rooted there in horror and could not stir. But iron slippers had already been set over a coal fire, and now that they had been brought to a red heat they were carried into the room with tongs and set down before the wicked queen. And she was made to put on the red-hot shoes, and to dance in them until she fell down dead.

Snow White by Kikuchi Kan (Aozora Bunko)
