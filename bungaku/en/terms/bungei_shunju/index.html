What is Bungei Shunjū?

Category
Society / Group

A monthly magazine founded in January 1923 by the writer Kikuchi Kan, funded from his own pocket. Conceived as a place for free comment unconstrained by the literary establishment, its inaugural issue opened with Akutagawa Ryūnosuke's essay "Shuju no Kotoba" ("Words of a Dwarf"), and it became a venue for essays and creative writing by a great many writers, including Kume Masao, Kawabata Yasunari, and Kobayashi Hideo. In 1935 Kikuchi established the Akutagawa and Naoki Prizes in memory of Akutagawa Ryūnosuke, who had died young, and of Naoki Sanjūgo, who had died suddenly the same year, and built the system, still carried on today by the successor Nihon Bungaku Shinkōkai foundation, that administers them. As a general-interest magazine it wielded great influence in the world of opinion and letters through the Taishō and Shōwa eras, and it remains the parent body of the still-publishing Bungei Shunjū company today.
