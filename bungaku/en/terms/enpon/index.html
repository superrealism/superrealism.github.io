What is Enpon?

Category
Other

The publishing format and boom, beginning with the "Gendai Nihon Bungaku Zenshū" ("Complete Collection of Modern Japanese Literature"), whose publication began in November 1926, of literary anthologies sold uniformly at one yen per volume, launched by Yamamoto Sanehiko, president of the Kaizōsha publishing company. The first installment delivered the month after publication began, the "Ozaki Kōyō Shū" (a volume of Ozaki Kōyō's works), drew an astonishing three hundred thousand-odd advance subscribers for its time, and pulled the near-bankrupt Kaizōsha back from the brink. The name is said to derive from the one-yen flat-fare taxis, called "entaku," then running in Osaka and Tokyo. This success prompted other publishers to follow with anthologies of their own, touching off a great boom in the publishing world of the early Shōwa era. The spread of inexpensive anthologies brought literary works, until then largely confined to a limited readership, into ordinary households across the country, and gave a strong push toward the popularization of reading culture, including popular fiction.
