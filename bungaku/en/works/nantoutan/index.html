About this work

A group of works that grew out of Nakajima Atsushi's posting to Palau as a textbook editor for the South Seas Bureau. It is the title portion of the 1942 collection of the same name, and consists of three pieces — "Happiness," "Husband and Wife" and "The Chickens." Retellings of Palauan folk tales and observations of island custom are mixed together, and an author known for a dignified Chinese-flavoured style sets down his eye for the country and people of the southern islands exactly as it was. Neither consuming another culture as a curiosity nor simply prettifying it, it has a peculiar flavour in which sober observation and bewilderment live side by side. All three pieces are carried here separately.
