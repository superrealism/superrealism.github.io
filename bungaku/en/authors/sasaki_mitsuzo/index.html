Biography

Born in 1896 in Shimotsugu Village, Kitashitara District, Aichi Prefecture (now Shitara Town); his real name was Mitsuzō, written with different characters. He graduated from the department of politics and economics at Meiji University and at first wrote serious short fiction, but, unable to win recognition, found his way by turning to popular literature. Kondō Umon, the taciturn master constable of "Umon Torimonochō" ("The Casebook of Umon"), and Saotome Mondonosuke, the scar-browed hero of "Hatamoto Taikutsu Otoko" ("The Bored Bannerman"), both became national heroes played again and again in film and on television. Straining his health with overwork, he died of acute pneumonia in 1934 at the age of 37.
