About this work

One of the brightest of all the Brothers Grimm's tales, in which a donkey, a dog, a cat and a cock, grown old and judged of no further use by their masters, set out to join the town band of Bremen. In Kusuyama Masao's version. They never reach Bremen at all, but take over a robbers' house they come upon on the way with the "music" they make piled one on another's back and all crying out at once. The idea of creatures no longer of any use banding together to make a place of their own is still taken up in picture books and on the stage all over the world.

Summary

A donkey who has carried heavy sacks to the mill for many long years realizes, now that he is old, that his master grudges the feeding of him, and runs away to offer himself to the town band of Bremen. On the road he meets a hound who can no longer run and was nearly beaten to death, a cat who can no longer catch mice and was nearly drowned, and a cock who has learned that he is to be made into soup tomorrow. "Come with us and make music at Bremen," and the four set off together; but they cannot get there that day and must pass the night in the forest. Guided by a light the cock sees from a high tree, they come to a house where a band of robbers has spread out a feast. The four pile themselves up, the dog on the donkey's back, the cat on the dog and the cock on top of all, and raise their voices together through the window. The robbers scatter, crying that a monster is upon them, and the four eat their fill and fall asleep. At midnight one robber comes back to see how things are, and in the dark is clawed in the face by the cat, bitten in the leg by the dog, kicked by the donkey, and flees home mistaking the cock's cry for a judge shouting. From then on the robbers never came near the house again, and the four liked it so well that they settled there for good.

Characters

  • The donkey　Grown old and cast off by his master, he takes it into his head to join the Bremen band
  • The dog　A hound who can no longer run. He joins the donkey on the road
  • The cat　An old cat who can no longer catch mice
  • The cock　He has run away on learning that he is to be made into soup tomorrow
  • The robbers　The band who had spread out a feast in the house in the forest

The opening (original text)

A man had a donkey that had carried sacks to the mill for many long years without complaint; but now its strength was at an end, and it grew less and less fit for work. So the master began to think of doing away with its feed. The donkey, however, saw that no good wind was blowing, and ran off, and set out on the road for Bremen; there, he thought, he might get himself taken on as a town musician.

The ending (original text)

"And up on the roof there sat the judge, who shouted, 'Bring me the rogue here!' So I made off as fast as I could." From then on the robbers never dared go into the house again; but the four Bremen musicians liked it there so well that they would not leave it. And the mouth of the man who told this last is warm still.
